README — Archive of Nika Sandler
(Prepared for long-term preservation)

If you are reading this text, some time has passed.
This archive was created to outlive platforms, technologies,
political regimes, and the life of its author.

────────────────────────────────

AUTHOR

Nika Sandler
Contemporary artist working at the intersection of photography,
post-photography and artificial intelligence.

This archive contains artistic works and texts produced in the early
21st century.

────────────────────────────────

TIME AND CONTEXT

The works in this archive were created during a period marked by:
– rapid expansion of artificial intelligence,
– digital surveillance and censorship in many parts of the world,
– ecological anxiety and planetary instability,
– a growing interest in archives, non-human agency, and
  alternative forms of memory.

Many projects reflect these conditions directly or indirectly.

────────────────────────────────

STRUCTURE OF THE ARCHIVE

This archive may include the following sections:
